Join us to celebrate the launch of the inaugural issue of The Love Letter magazine live at The George Tavern.

In this first issue, we asked contributors to explore the theme of EPHEMERA. To consider the significance of the short-lived and evanescent. From transient anuses to the ticking of time, from faltering of leaves to deep space probes, we hope these reflections of impermanence elicit your own observations on the brief and the momentary.

DOGSBODY

The brainchild of Mimi Jones, whose songwriting has been likened to Jeff Buckley, Elliott Smith and Mazzy Star, Dogsbody craft honest songs with grungy chord progressions; every note is purposeful and every lyric deliberate.

Mimi's jazz-imbued vocals and raw delivery bring the songs into their most intimate form.

OSCAR JONES

Oscar Jones is a Bristol-based folk songwriter known for his earnest and sophisticated songs.

He and his band create a raw wall of sound with janky guitars and naive sing-alongs. For fans of Tom Waits, Michael Hurley, and Belle and Sebastian.
